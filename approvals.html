Approval Guidelines

1. Upload → Validate: QA Coordinator checks metadata and completeness

2. Validate → Approve: Dean/QA Head reviews content and compliance

3. Approve → Lock: Final version is locked and published

4. Rejection: Returns to uploader with comments

⏱️ SLA: Validation within 48h · Approval within 72h
